About us

Ahya ul Quran Online Academy

Ahya ul Quran Online Academy provides online Quran education for children and adults around the world. Our aim is to help students learn the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation, Tajweed, and Islamic values through convenient online classes.

Bringing the Light of the Quran to Hearts Around the World.

Correct recitation

Makharij, Tajweed and clear pronunciation from the first lesson.

Learn from anywhere

Convenient live classes for families in every time zone.

Islamic values

Quran, Hadith and character taught together with care.
